Privacy Policy

KarmaJar · Effective date: October 12, 2026

KarmaJar ("the app", "we") is developed by David Quico. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account info: email address and password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password).
  • Profile: the display name you choose.
  • Game data: your groups and membership, points balance, rewards and penalties given and received, prize redemptions, and the custom reward, penalty and prize names your group creates.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe or buy an in-app purchase, Apple processes the payment. We receive purchase status (not your card details) through RevenueCat.
  • Technical data: basic diagnostic and usage information may be collected by our service providers to keep the app working.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or health data.

Anonymous rewards

When you send a reward anonymously, other group members never see who sent it. The sender is still stored on our servers so the app can enforce limits and prevent abuse.

How we use information

  • To run your account, groups, leaderboard, activity feed and prize shop.
  • To manage subscriptions and purchases.
  • To fix bugs, prevent abuse and improve the app.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information. KarmaJar points have no cash value.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) for accounts and data storage.
  • RevenueCat and Apple for subscriptions and purchases.

If we add advertising (for example AppLovin) in the future, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before it launches, and ask for your permission where required (such as Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt).

What other members see

Members of a group can see your display name, points, and the non-anonymous actions you take in that group. Groups are joined only with an invite code.

Children

KarmaJar can be used by families and classrooms, and includes a kid-safe mode. However, KarmaJar is not directed primarily at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. We have not obtained any COPPA certification or safe-harbor membership.

Children under 13 should only use KarmaJar with a parent, guardian or teacher who creates and manages the account. Teachers using KarmaJar in class should follow their school's policies. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information without consent, email us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data in the app (Settings → Delete account) or by emailing us. Some records may remain briefly in backups before being removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your data. Contact us to make a request.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
