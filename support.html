FAQ

How do I create a group?
Tap Create Group, set your rewards, penalties, prizes, starting points and daily max, then share the 6-character invite code.

How do I join a group?
Tap Join Group and enter the invite code from your host.

Are anonymous rewards really anonymous?
Yes. Other members never see who sent an anonymous reward.

Is there real money involved?
No. Points have no cash value. Prizes are whatever your group agrees on.

What is kid-safe mode?
A setting hosts can enable for a gentler experience with younger players. Children under 13 should use KarmaJar with a parent, guardian or teacher.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → KarmaJar. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

How do I restore purchases?
Go to Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my account?
In the app go to Settings → Delete account, or email us.

How do I report inappropriate content?
Tell your group host, or email us with the group name and details.
